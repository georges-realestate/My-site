Residency & citizenship

10 years

Golden Visa: with property

Own UAE property worth AED 2M or more, alone or combined. Mortgaged and qualifying off-plan property can count.

  • Sponsor spouse, children and parents
  • Valuation route if your property has grown past AED 2M
  • Visa moved to a new asset if you sell and reinvest
10 years

Golden Visa: without property

No property needed. Qualify through your capital, your business, your salary or your track record.

  • Capital: AED 2M in a UAE bank deposit, investment fund or company
  • Founders: a UAE business with around AED 1M in annual revenue, or a venture endorsed by a UAE authority
  • Professionals: a basic salary of AED 30,000+ a month, with an attested degree
  • Talent: doctors, scientists, executives, creatives, athletes, nurses, teachers and content creators, by official nomination
55+ · Renewable

Golden Visa for retirees

Retire in Dubai on your own sponsorship, backed by your property, your savings or your pension. A 5-year residence visa, renewable.

  • Eligibility: aged 55 or over, with at least 15 years of work history in the UAE or abroad
  • Property and savings: UAE property worth AED 1M+ together with AED 1M+ in savings
  • Income: a pension or fixed income of AED 240,000+ a year (AED 20,000 a month) to apply in Dubai
  • Own AED 2M+ in property? The 10-year Golden Visa may suit you better
