About Georges

A considered approach to every property decision.

For 25 years, Georges Akkaoui has helped businesses across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the wider Gulf make complex decisions with confidence.

His background in B2B technology, data, and market insight developed a disciplined way of reading markets, understanding stakeholder priorities, and finding common ground. Today, he brings that experience to commercial real estate in Dubai.

Buying, selling, leasing, or investing can have a lasting impact on a business or portfolio. Georges combines a data-informed perspective with patience in negotiation and a personal understanding of the people behind each decision.

Lebanese by heritage and a long-time Gulf resident, Georges is fluent in English, Arabic, and French. He is at home across cultures, with a deep appreciation for Dubai and the Khaleeji values of trust, discretion, and lasting relationships.

His aim is simple: to share what the data says, offer an honest view of the market, and help each client decide what is right for them.
